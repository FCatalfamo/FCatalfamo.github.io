About me.

Academic Profile

I am a PhD researcher in Political Science at the Network for the Advancement of Social and Political Studies (NASP), jointly affiliated with the University of Milan and the University of Pavia. Before starting my PhD, I completed an M.A. in Political Science at the University of Mannheim (current grade: 1.8) and an M.A. in Public and Corporate Communication at the University of Milan (110/110 cum laude), with a curriculum in Data Analytics for Politics, Society and Complex Organizations. I previously obtained a B.A. in Political Science from the University of Milan (104/110).

My research focuses on political behaviour, party politics and political communication, with particular interests in coalition politics, affective polarization and intra-party dynamics. I primarily use quantitative and comparative methods, including panel data and computational approaches to political text.

Professional Experience
Istituto Bruno Leoni
Research & Communication

At the Istituto Bruno Leoni, my work combined research and policy analysis with the communication of research to broader audiences. Alongside quantitative work, data collection and research support, I contributed to translating policy research into accessible public-facing content.

My work included social media and digital communication, press releases, graphics, video and audio production, website management, stakeholder outreach and event organisation. I was also involved in educational initiatives aimed at bringing economic and policy debates to students outside traditional academic settings.
