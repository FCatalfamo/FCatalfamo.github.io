About me.

Academic Profile

I am a political scientist interested in how political parties compete, cooperate and communicate, and in how citizens respond to these dynamics.

I am currently a PhD researcher in Political Science at the Network for the Advancement of Social and Political Studies (NASP), jointly affiliated with the University of Milan and the University of Pavia. Before starting my PhD, I studied Political Science at the University of Mannheim and Public and Corporate Communication at the University of Milan, where I followed a curriculum in Data Analytics for Politics, Society and Complex Organizations.

My research focuses primarily on political behaviour, party politics, political communication, coalition politics and affective polarization. I mainly work with quantitative and comparative research designs, including panel data, causal inference and computational approaches to political text.

Professional Experience
Istituto Bruno Leoni
Research & Communication

Alongside academic research, I have worked at the Istituto Bruno Leoni on the production and communication of policy research. My work combined quantitative analysis and research support with the broader task of translating research findings into accessible public-facing content.

A substantial part of my work focused on research communication: developing content for social media, preparing press releases and other communication materials, producing graphics, video and audio content, managing web content and supporting the dissemination of research across different audiences.

I have also been involved in stakeholder outreach, event organisation and educational activities, including work on initiatives aimed at bringing economic and policy debates to students outside traditional academic settings.
