About me.

Academic Profile

I am a political scientist interested in political behaviour, party politics and political communication.

My research focuses on how political cooperation, competition and elite behaviour shape citizens' attitudes toward parties and politics, with particular attention to affective polarization, coalition politics and intra-party dynamics.

I mainly work with quantitative and comparative methods, combining panel data, causal inference strategies and computational approaches to political text.

Background

My academic background combines political science, communication and data analysis.

Before beginning my PhD, I studied Political Science at the University of Mannheim and Public and Corporate Communication at the University of Milan, where I followed a curriculum focused on data analytics for politics, society and complex organizations.

This interdisciplinary path continues to shape the way I approach political questions, particularly those involving political behaviour, communication and empirical measurement.
