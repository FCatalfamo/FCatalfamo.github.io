Profile

I am a PhD researcher in Political Science at the Network for the Advancement of Social and Political Studies (NASP), jointly affiliated with the University of Milan and the University of Pavia.

My research interests lie at the intersection of political behaviour, party politics and political communication, with a particular focus on affective polarization, coalition politics and citizens’ responses to elite behaviour. I mainly use quantitative and comparative methods.
